## What you can do

- Search any song and sing it with lyrics that follow the music, with karaoke versions picked automatically when they exist
- Live scoring while you sing, in Solo, Duet and Tournament modes
- Rooms with friends: shared queue, scoreboard, voice chat and "vote to skip"
- Playlists, favourites and your singing history on your device
- Hear-yourself echo and reverb when singing without a room

## Installing

**Windows.** Run the installer. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**
(the app is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows does not know it yet). After the first install the app **updates itself**:
new versions download quietly and install the next time you close the app.

**Android.** Open the `.apk` file. Android will ask you to allow installing apps from this source once. To update, download the newest
`.apk` and open it again; your settings stay.

## Good to know

- Karaoke Night needs an **internet connection**: the songs, lyrics and rooms with friends run online.
- It asks for **microphone** access to score your singing. Your microphone is only used while a song is playing.
- Songs play through YouTube’s official embedded player and lyrics come from LRCLIB. Nothing is downloaded or stored.
  Karaoke Night is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, YouTube Music or LRCLIB.
